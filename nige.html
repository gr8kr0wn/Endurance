01 · Project overviewMaking a Web3 ecosystem easy to understand

Nige is a Web3 ecosystem built around learning, nesting and predictions. During my time working with the project as an influencer, I helped introduce its offerings to potential users, communicate its value and build interest across online communities.

02 · My roleRepresented Nige where conversations happen

I represented Nige across X, Telegram and WhatsApp, creating written content, engaging with community members and helping interested people understand the ecosystem.

03 · My approachListen first, then write

Rather than simply promoting the project, I paid attention to how people discussed it, studied previous conversations and observed the questions, interests and pain points of the audience.

I used these insights to shape my content, explain Nige’s offerings and respond to people who wanted to learn more.— Research before promotion

I also built relationships with interested users through direct conversations and community engagement, helping guide them towards joining the ecosystem.

04 · Key contributionsWhat I did

  • Created written promotional and educational content for Nige.
  • Represented the project across X, Telegram and WhatsApp.
  • Researched audience conversations and tailored content to their interests.
  • Engaged with potential users and answered questions about the ecosystem.
  • Supported onboarding by directing interested people to relevant Nige offerings.
  • Promoted NigeLearn when it launched.

05 · Results & impactContent that moved people to act

90confirmed sign-ups/referrals generated through a viral NigeLearn promotional post.
Referral dashboard: 90 confirmed members.
  • Generated thousands of views across content published for Nige.
  • Attracted direct enquiries from people interested in the project and helped onboard them.
  • Community members shared their own wins publicly, including a first $100 earned on Nige (shown in the collage above).

06 · What this demonstratesResearch + storytelling + community

My work with Nige reflects my ability to combine audience research, storytelling, content creation and community engagement to make a project more understandable and encourage people to take action.
