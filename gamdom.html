01 · Project overviewA promo that plays out like a story

Gamdom challenged creators to promote the platform through real-life content. Rather than produce a conventional promotional video, I developed an original concept built around a fictional Gamdom takeover, using a news broadcast, newspaper presentation and telephone conversation to introduce the platform and its offerings.

Context: this was an entry to Gamdom’s public creator challenge, not a paid client campaign.

02 · My roleIndependent Creative Director, Scriptwriter & Video Producer

I handled the entire project from concept development to final editing, working independently as a solo creator.

03 · My creative approachEntertainment first, advertisement second

I wanted to make the promotion feel like an entertaining story rather than a straightforward advertisement.

I developed the storyline, wrote the script, planned the scenes and designed the visual elements around the takeover concept.

The newspaperA newspaper-style presentation sets the scene: Gamdom has taken over the headlines.
The broadcastA fictional radio news broadcast carries the story forward.
The phone callA telephone conversation introduces Gamdom’s Originals, Casino, Sports and Exclusives.
The worldProps, set and visuals were all designed to make the takeover feel real.

04 · Production & executionEvery part, done solo

  • Concept development and storytelling.
  • Scriptwriting and scene planning.
  • Prop sourcing and set preparation.
  • Filming and camera positioning.
  • Video editing, sound design and colour grading.
Behind the scenes

One of the biggest production challenges was finding a suitable telephone for the storyline. After asking around and searching for some time, I eventually found one at a gadget store rather than compromise on the concept.
