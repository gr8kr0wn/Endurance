01 · Project overviewWhat happens after a project wins attention?

While exploring emerging Web3 projects, I discovered Sovra, a self-custodial global dollar account powered by USDC. Intrigued by what the project was building, I decided to examine its positioning, messaging and approach to attracting potential users.

This independent analysis explored how Sovra captured attention and identified opportunities to sustain audience interest beyond initial discovery.

If 50,000 people discovered your project today, how many would still care about it six months from now?— From the article

02 · My research approachStudying the journey as a real user

I began by studying Sovra’s website, founder’s account and presence across other social media platforms to understand its product, messaging and communication strategy.

I also experienced the onboarding journey firsthand by joining its waitlist. Although I received an immediate confirmation email, there were no subsequent updates to nurture my interest.

This experience prompted me to examine what happens after a project successfully attracts attention.

03 · Key observationStrong curiosity, quiet follow-through

Sovra’s messaging created curiosity by addressing broader questions around money and ownership rather than focusing solely on product features.

However, I identified an opportunity to strengthen the journey between initial interest and long-term engagement. People who joined the waitlist had already demonstrated interest in the product. Without consistent communication, education and updates, that attention could gradually fade.

04 · My strategic recommendationsWays to nurture and retain interest

Email marketingConsistent communication through educational updates, founder notes and product announcements.
Educational contentContent around self-custody, digital dollars, money ownership and practical use cases.
Community buildingGive interested users opportunities to connect, learn and engage with the project’s vision.
Audience researchUnderstand what potential users want to know, what excites them and what questions remain unanswered.
Continuous engagementMaintain meaningful communication before and after launch, giving users reasons to stay connected.
Note: these recommendations come from my own independent analysis. They were not commissioned by Sovra, and I’m not claiming Sovra implemented them.

05 · My contributionResearch, writing and design

I independently researched the project, analysed its messaging and user journey, developed the article’s central argument and wrote the analysis.

I also designed the accompanying graphics to communicate the ideas visually and strengthen the article’s presentation.
